TOOLS OF SURVIVAL

RUST WEAPONS

Weapons help players defend themselves and survive. Their crafting costs and statistics may change after updates.

Tier 1 Workbench Weapons

These weapons are generally part of early progression. The order below is a simple guide from basic weapons to more advanced Tier 1 choices. Use the current in-game tech tree to confirm exact unlock costs and recipes.

01. Eoka Pistol

Workbench: No workbench required for crafting.

Crafting materials: 75 Wood, 30 Metal Fragments.

Weapon type: Primitive pistol. Uses Handmade Shells.

Statistics: Damage depends on ammunition and hit location; it is a single-shot weapon with a slow reload.

02. Waterpipe Shotgun

Workbench: Level 1 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 150 Wood, 75 Metal Fragments.

Weapon type: Single-shot shotgun. Uses shotgun ammunition.

Statistics: Strong at close range, but must be reloaded after each shot.

03. Revolver

Workbench: Level 1 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 Metal Pipe, 25 Cloth, 125 Metal Fragments.

Weapon type: Pistol. Uses Pistol Bullets.

Statistics: Six-round cylinder and moderate firing speed. Damage changes with range and hit location.

04. Double Barrel Shotgun

Workbench: Level 1 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 175 Metal Fragments, 2 Metal Pipes.

Weapon type: Shotgun. Holds two shots before reloading.

Statistics: Very dangerous at close range; less effective at long range.

05. Semi-Automatic Pistol (SAP)

Workbench: Level 2 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 Semi Automatic Body, 1 Metal Pipe, 6 High Quality Metal.

Weapon type: Semi-automatic pistol. Uses Pistol Bullets.

Statistics: Faster follow-up shots than the Revolver; damage depends on distance and hit location.

Tier 2 Workbench Weapons

Tier 2 weapons offer more reliable performance and are commonly used after players collect better components.

06. Custom SMG

Workbench: Level 2 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 SMG Body, 1 Metal Pipe, 8 High Quality Metal.

Weapon type: Submachine gun. Uses Pistol Bullets.

Statistics: High rate of fire and a 24-round magazine. Recoil makes longer-range shooting harder.

07. Thompson

Workbench: Level 2 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 SMG Body, 10 High Quality Metal, 100 Wood, 1 Metal Spring

Weapon type: Submachine gun. Uses Pistol Bullets.

Statistics: 20-round magazine and a fast rate of fire. Effective in close and medium-range fights.

08. Semi-Automatic Rifle (SAR)

Workbench: Level 2 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 Semi Automatic Body, 450 Metal Fragments, 4 High Quality Metal, 1 Metal Spring

Weapon type: Semi-automatic rifle. Uses Rifle Ammunition.

Statistics: Accurate follow-up shots and a 16-round magazine; good for medium-range combat.

09. Pump Shotgun

Workbench: Level 2 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 2 Metal Pipes, 15 High Quality Metal, 1 Metal Spring

Weapon type: Pump-action shotgun.

Statistics: Strong close-range damage and multiple shots before reloading; limited range.

Tier 3 Workbench Weapons

These weapons are associated with later progression and usually require rarer components or more advanced crafting access.

10. MP5A4

Workbench: Level 3 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 SMG Body, 15 High Quality Metal, 2 Metal Spring.

Weapon type: Submachine gun. Uses Pistol Bullets.

Statistics: High rate of fire and a 30-round magazine; best used with recoil control.

11. Bolt Action Rifle

Workbench: Level 3 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 Rifle Body, 3 Metal Pipes, 30 High Quality Metal, 1 Metal Spring.

Weapon type: Bolt-action rifle. Uses Rifle Ammunition.

Statistics: High damage at long range, but the shooter must cycle the bolt between shots.

12. Assault Rifle (AK)

Workbench: Level 3 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 1 Rifle Body, 4 Metal Pipes, 50 High Quality Metal, 50 woods.

Weapon type: Automatic rifle. Uses Rifle Ammunition.

Statistics: 30-round magazine and strong damage. Recoil control is important during automatic fire.

Throwables

Throwables are listed after firearms because they have different uses and do not all follow the same weapon workbench progression.

13. F1 Grenade

Workbench: Level 2 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 30 Gun Powder, 25 Metal Fragments.

Use: Explosive throwable used to damage opponents.

Statistics: Blast effect depends on distance and cover. Keep a safe distance from the explosion.

14. Beancan Grenade

Workbench: Level 1 Workbench.

Crafting materials: 60 Metal Fragments, 20 Gun Powder.

Use: A low-tier explosive throwable, also used in some explosive crafting recipes.

Statistics: Has a chance to fail to detonate immediately, so do not treat its timing as fully predictable.

15. Incendiary Bottle

Workbench: Level 2 Workbench.

Crafting materials:10 Cloth, 50 Low Grade Fuel

Use: Creates fire can break building build from wood.

Statistics: Mainly a utility item rather than a direct-damage weapon.
