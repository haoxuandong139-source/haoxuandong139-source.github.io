THE WORLD OF RUST

THE UNKNOWN

An abandoned world filled with ruins, secrets, and the struggle to survive.

A Mysterious World

The world of Rust is filled with abandoned buildings, industrial ruins, military bases, and research facilities. These locations suggest that people once built a developed society, but much of the world is now empty and dangerous.

01. The Survivors

Players arrive in the world with very few supplies. They must gather resources, find food, craft tools, and build shelters. They can work together with other survivors, but they may also become enemies.

02. Scientists and Military Sites

Scientists guard several important locations, including research facilities and military sites. These places contain valuable resources and advanced equipment. Their existence raises questions about what happened before the players arrived.

03. The R.U.S.T Mystery

The name R.U.S.T. has been used in official content as "Repopulation Unit Survival Test." This suggests a possible connection to a survival test or population program. However, the full purpose of this setting has not been completely explained.

04. What Happened to the World?

Rust does not have one complete, confirmed story explaining every ruin or why the world became this way. Players explore the environment and discover clues for themselves. The mystery is part of what makes the game interesting.

Can You Survive?

In Rust, trust is dangerous, resources are valuable, and survival is never guaranteed. Every player creates their own story.
