RUST MONUMENTS

Monuments contain useful loot, puzzles, and keycards. Explore carefully and prepare for radiation.

Keycard Progression

Rust has three main keycard colours: Green, Blue, and Red. Players often start with a Green Keycard and use monument puzzles to reach higher levels of loot. Many puzzles also need an Electric Fuse.

01. Green Keycard

Where to find it: Desks and shelves at places such as gas stations, supermarkets, and some low-tier monuments. Exact spawn locations can vary.

Radiation: There is no single radiation value for every green-card location. Some locations have little radiation; others may need protection.

Use: Opens green-card puzzle doors and helps players begin the keycard progression.

Refresh time: The card itself and monument loot use different spawn rules. Ordinary loot often has a timer around one hour, but server settings can change it.

02. Blue Keycard

Where to find it: Commonly obtained from green-card puzzle reward rooms, including routes at Sewer Branch, Satellite Dish, or Harbor, depending on the monument layout.

Radiation: Depends on the monument and the specific room. Bring suitable radiation protection when required.

Use: Opens blue-card puzzle doors and provides access to better loot and some routes to Red Keycards.

Refresh time: Puzzle loot and ordinary containers may use separate timers. Around one hour is a general guide for many ordinary loot containers, not a universal rule.

03. Red Keycard

Where to find it: Often obtained from blue-card puzzle reward rooms at monuments such as Airfield, Power Plant, Train Yard, or Water Treatment Plant, depending on the current route.

Radiation: Advanced monuments may have high-radiation areas. The required protection depends on the exact route and room.

Use: Opens red-card doors, which can lead to high-tier crates and valuable components.

Refresh time: Puzzle rooms have their own reset rules. Ordinary container timers and puzzle resets are not necessarily the same.

Important Monuments

These locations are useful for finding crates, components, and keycard puzzles. Radiation and loot rules can differ by server.

04. Sewer Branch

Keycard: Commonly part of the Green Keycard progression.

Radiation: Low to moderate in different areas; check the route before entering.

Use: Loot crates and components, and a route toward Blue Keycard rewards.

Refresh time: Container respawns and puzzle resets may differ. Server settings can change both.

05. Airfield

Keycard: Contains puzzle routes that may use Green and Blue Keycards.

Radiation: Some sections have radiation; the exact amount depends on the area.

Use: Loot crates, collect components, and progress through puzzle rooms.

Refresh time: Regular loot containers often use a timer around one hour. Puzzle resets may work differently.

06. Power Plant

Keycard: Has Blue Keycard puzzle progression in the usual monument route.

Radiation: Some parts have moderate or high radiation, so protection may be needed.

Use: Find crates and components, and access puzzle rewards.

Refresh time: Regular containers and puzzle rooms can have different reset timers.

07. Train Yard

Keycard: Includes keycard puzzle progression, depending on the route.

Radiation: Radiation levels vary by area.

Use: Loot crates and components, and work toward higher-tier puzzle rewards.

Refresh time: Ordinary loot may respawn around one hour after being looted, but server settings can change this.

08. Water Treatment Plant

Keycard: Commonly part of Blue Keycard puzzle progression.

Radiation: Different sections have different radiation levels; prepare before entering restricted areas.

Use: Search crates and components and complete puzzle rooms.

Refresh time: Regular containers and puzzle rewards may follow separate reset rules.

09. Launch Site

Keycard: An advanced monument with a complex puzzle route. Requirements should be checked for the current layout.

Radiation: Some areas have high radiation. Radiation protection is important for the hazardous route.

Use: High-tier crates and valuable loot, with greater risk from radiation and other players.

Refresh time: Loot-container respawns and puzzle resets can differ by server and game update.

Plan Before You Enter

Radiation values, card requirements, and respawn times can change with updates and server settings. Always check the current monument route and bring suitable protection.
